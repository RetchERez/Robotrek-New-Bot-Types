# Robotrek: New Bot Types v1.0

Three robot classes bring different combat strengths, permanent starting minimums and class labels to the original Robotrek campaign.

- INFILTRATOR favors melee damage and gains one movement tile
- HUNTER favors guns and lasers, with one extra recovery unit for gun actions and gun-containing RUN programs, capped at 12
- JUGGERNAUT favors bombs and starts with more Energy and Guard, with one fewer movement tile
- Class modifiers apply to normal attacks and RUN programs using the actual equipped weapon families
- Native roster, status, equipment and Program/Skill screens identify your robot's class
- The stat-allocation/body-color screen identifies the robot class above its name
- Owned classes are greyed out and cannot be confirmed; their previews remain available
- The purchase selector previews all five starting stats, sword/gun/bomb multipliers and the highlighted class's penalty
- Equipment and Program/Skill class labels follow their screens' top-left rivets; the equipment label sits above the robot name
- Class storage preserves all eight third-robot programs and their names through native saving, loading and roster ordering
